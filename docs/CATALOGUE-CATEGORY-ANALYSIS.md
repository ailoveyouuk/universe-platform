# Catalogue category analysis: how global-health buyers classify what they buy

Prepared 2026-10-24 for the Universe catalogue extension. Compares Universe's 10 product groups with the categories published by CRS, WHO, UNICEF, UNFPA, FHI 360, the Global Fund, USAID GHSC-PSM, the World Bank, ADB and NATO (NSPA).

## 1. What each organisation publishes

| Organisation | How it classifies | Categories seen (as published) |
|---|---|---|
| **UNICEF** (2020 Supply Annual Report; $3.23bn goods) | Eight commodity groups | Vaccines/biologicals $1,413m; Medical supplies and equipment $831m (health kits, immunisation syringes, safety boxes, HIV and malaria RDTs); Water and sanitation $183m (purification tablets, chlorination sachets, hygiene kits); Nutrition $172m (RUTF, vitamin A, deworming, micronutrient powder, iron/folic acid); Pharmaceuticals $144m; Cold chain equipment $104m (of which $67m solar); Education supplies $43m; Bed nets $36m (17.8m LLINs) |
| **UNFPA** (2023; $539m) | Fifteen commodity/service categories | Contraceptives 38%; Dignity, inter-agency reproductive health and other kits 13%; Travel/events 6%; Freight, handling, storage and customs 6%; Management/evaluation/admin services 6%; IT and communications 5%; Medical devices 5%; Premises/construction 4%; Pharmaceuticals 3%; Vehicles 2%; Office furniture and supplies 2%; Printing/promotion 2%; Sampling, inspection and testing under 1% |
| **FHI 360** (quality-control scope) | Product list | Antimalarials; ARVs; food by prescription; instrument-based diagnostics; IUDs; laboratory supplies; LLINs; male and female condoms; microbicides; oral and injectable contraceptives; other essential and strategic medicines; PPE; RDT kits; TB preventive treatment; VMMC and other procedural kits |
| **Global Fund** (Pooled Procurement Mechanism) | Core and non-core products | Core: ARVs, HIV RDTs and viral load tests, ACTs, LLINs, other antimalarials, malaria RDTs. | **USAID GHSC-PSM** | Five health areas | Reproductive health (injectables, implants, pills, copper and hormonal IUDs, emergency contraception, condoms); HIV/AIDS (first/second-line adult and paediatric ARVs, lab commodities and instruments, condoms, VMMC commodities); Malaria (ACTs, RDTs, SP, severe malaria medicines, LLINs); Maternal, newborn and child health (including PPH medicines); Zika and emerging issues |
| **WHO** (prequalification, the reference list buyers use) | Product streams | In vitro diagnostics; medicines (finished products, APIs, QC laboratories); vaccines; immunisation devices; vector control products; male circumcision devices; cold chain equipment; medical devices. About 1,500 products prequalified |
| **CRS** (vendor page, 2026) | Five vendor areas | Emergency shelter materials; household kits; WASH items; tool kits; logistics and warehousing services (including transport) |
| **World Bank** | Procurement type, not product | Goods; Works; Non-consulting services; Consulting services. | **ADB** | Procurement type and sector | Same four procurement types. Health was 17% of 2022 ADB-financed procurement (about $1.6bn of $9.5bn); the 2022 decline was driven by lower vaccine and health-supply needs. No commodity breakdown was available in what I could read |

## 2. Coverage against Universe's 10 groups

Universe today: Pharmaceuticals, Diagnostics, Reagents, Laboratory Consumables, Medical Devices, Surgical Supplies, Wound Care, Imaging, Personal Protective Equipment, Cold Chain.

| Category buyers use | Where it sits in Universe today | Seen at | Gap |
|---|---|---|---|
| Vaccines and immunisation devices | Sub-category under Pharmaceuticals; syringes under Surgical Supplies | UNICEF (largest line, 44%), WHO, NSPA | Under-weighted. Consider a top-level Immunisation group (vaccines, auto-disable syringes, safety boxes) |

| Kits (reproductive health, dignity, health, PEP, VMMC, household, tool) | None | UNFPA 13%, UNICEF, FHI 360, CRS, Global Fund | Gap. Kits are multi-line bundles |

| Water, sanitation and hygiene | None | UNICEF $183m, CRS | Gap |

## 3. Findings

1. **The large health commodity flows are well covered** (medicines, diagnostics, devices, PPE, cold chain, lab). The biggest hole is **reproductive health** and **kits**, which together are about half of UNFPA's spend, then **nutrition**, **WASH** and **vector control**.
2. **Vaccines are the biggest single line** at UNICEF. Universe treats them as one pharmaceutical sub-category; the buyers treat them as a category with its own devices and cold chain.
3. **The multilateral banks classify differently.** The World Bank and ADB sort by procurement type (goods, works, non-consulting, consulting) and sector, never by commodity. Tenders under their financing need a procurement-type field alongside product category.
4. **Services are a real share of spend.** UNFPA's categories show freight, storage and customs at 6% and management/evaluation services at 6%; CRS lists logistics as a vendor area; NSPA lists medical services and servicing. The catalogue is product-only today.
5. **Shared reference lists exist.** WHO prequalification streams, ATC codes for medicines, HS codes (already used) and UNSPSC (used on the UN Global Marketplace) are what buyers cross-reference.

## 4. Recommended additions, updated by this evidence

| Priority | Group | Evidence | Suggested sub-categories |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Reproductive health and family planning** | UNFPA 38%, GHSC-PSM, FHI 360, Global Fund | Condoms; IUDs; implants; injectables; oral and emergency contraceptives; maternal health kits |
| 2 | **Nutrition** | UNICEF $172m, FHI 360 | RUTF; micronutrient powders and supplements; vitamin A and deworming; fortified foods; infant feeding |
| 3 | **Vector control** | UNICEF, FHI 360, Global Fund, WHO, GHSC-PSM | LLINs; IRS insecticides; larvicides; spraying equipment |
| 4 | **Water, sanitation and hygiene** | UNICEF $183m, CRS | Purification tablets and filters; chlorination; hygiene and handwashing kits; sanitation items |
| 5 | **Immunisation** (elevate from Pharmaceuticals) | UNICEF 44%, WHO | Vaccines; auto-disable syringes; safety boxes; vaccine-specific cold chain links |
| 6 | **Kits and emergency supplies** | UNFPA 13%, UNICEF, CRS, NSPA | Inter-agency emergency health kits; dignity/hygiene kits; PEP and VMMC kits; household kits; tool kits; trauma kits |
| 7 | **Shelter and household items** | CRS | Emergency shelter; tents; blankets; household sets |
| 8 | **Field and military medical** | NSPA | Field hospital modules; resuscitation and patient transport; first aid |
| 9 | **Logistics and services** (separate service catalogue) | UNFPA, CRS, NSPA | Freight; warehousing; customs; biomedical servicing; sampling, inspection and testing |

Earlier suggestions that the evidence supports less: dental and ophthalmic, health IT, and waste management did not appear in any of the published category lists I could read. They are real needs but lower priority.

## 5. Limits of this analysis

- Published category data is thin for **PATH** (none found), **CRS** (vendor areas only), **ADB** (sector level only; the goods table was not readable) and **NSPA** (descriptive article; its formal commodity classification could not be read).
- UNICEF figures are from the 2020 report; UNFPA from 2023. Shares will have shifted.
- UNSPSC segment names were confirmed but numeric codes were not on the page I read; verify before coding to them.
- WHO has no single procurement category list; the WHO prequalification streams are the best proxy.
